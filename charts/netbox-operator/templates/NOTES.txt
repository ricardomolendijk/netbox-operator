CRDs: Helm installs charts/netbox-operator/crds/ once and never upgrades them. After
upgrading this release, upgrade the CRDs yourself:

  make upgrade-crds        # or: kubectl apply --server-side -f <chart>/crds/

`helm uninstall` deliberately leaves them, because deleting a CRD deletes every CR of that
kind and their finalizers would then delete the NetBox objects. docs/install.md.
